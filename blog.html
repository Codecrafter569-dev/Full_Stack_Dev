Aardvark (ऐर्व

The Aardvark (ऐर्डवर्क) is one of the most unusual mammals in the world. It is a nocturnal (रात्रिचर) animal that lives in Africa and feeds mainly on ants and termites. Because of its long snout, powerful claws, and pig-like body, it looks like a mixture of several animals, but it belongs to its own unique group. The word "Aardvark" comes from the Dutch/Afrikaans language and means "Earth Pig" (धरती का सूअर). Although it resembles an anteater, pig, or armadillo, it is not closely related to any of them. Scientific Name: Orycteropus afer Meaning: "Orycteropus" = Digging foot (खोदने वाला पैर) "afer" = African (अफ्रीकी)
